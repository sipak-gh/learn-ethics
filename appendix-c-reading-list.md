## Part 3 — A graduate-level path

[Chapter 22](22-building-your-ethics.md#a-graduate-reading-path) sets out a seven-stage path (the classics read whole, the twentieth-century foundations, normative theory today, metaethics, moral psychology, applied and political ethics, and traditions beyond the Western canon). This part adds the reference works, anthologies, secondary literature and journals that let you enter the scholarly conversation on any chapter.

### Reference works (free online)

Read the encyclopedia entry before the monographs: it maps the positions and names the scholars who hold them.

- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy:** [Morality, The Definition of](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/morality-definition/); [Reflective Equilibrium](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/reflective-equilibrium/); [Virtue Ethics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-virtue/); [Kant's Moral Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kant-moral/); [Consequentialism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/consequentialism/); [Contractualism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/contractualism/); [Feminist Ethics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/feminism-ethics/); [Nietzsche's Moral and Political Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nietzsche-moral-political/); [Existentialism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/existentialism/); [Metaethics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/metaethics/); [Moral Realism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/moral-realism/); [Moral Anti-Realism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/moral-anti-realism/); [Moral Responsibility](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/moral-responsibility/); [Moral Psychology: Empirical Approaches](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/moral-psych-emp/); [Theory and Bioethics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/theory-bioethics/); [Environmental Ethics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-environmental/); [Ethics of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-ai/); [Chinese Ethics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-chinese/); [African Ethics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/african-ethics/).
- **Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy:** [Ethics](https://iep.utm.edu/ethics/), a long survey of the whole field; [Care Ethics](https://iep.utm.edu/care-ethics/); [Non-Cognitivism in Ethics](https://iep.utm.edu/non-cogn/).
- **PhilPapers** ([philpapers.org](https://philpapers.org/)) indexes the journal literature by topic; its "Normative Ethics", "Meta-Ethics" and "Applied Ethics" categories are the place to find recent work.
- The SEP's *Archives* (plato.stanford.edu/archives/) keep fixed, citable editions of every entry; cite those in your own work.

### Anthologies and handbooks

- Russ Shafer-Landau (ed.), *Ethical Theory: An Anthology* (Wiley-Blackwell, 2nd ed. 2013): the standard graduate collection of normative theory.
- Stephen Darwall, Allan Gibbard and Peter Railton (eds.), *Moral Discourse and Practice: Some Philosophical Approaches* (Oxford, 1997): the classic metaethics reader.
- Russ Shafer-Landau and Terence Cuneo (eds.), *Foundations of Ethics: An Anthology* (Blackwell, 2007): metaethics, with the major papers from Moore to the present.
- David Copp (ed.), *The Oxford Handbook of Ethical Theory* (Oxford, 2006).
