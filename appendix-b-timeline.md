# Appendix B — Timeline

The history of ethics from Confucius and Socrates to today, with the chapter that covers each event. Ancient dates are approximate, and several (especially for early Chinese and Indian thinkers) are traditional or disputed; works are dated by first publication unless noted. Previous: [Appendix A — Glossary](appendix-a-glossary.md). Next: [Appendix C — Reading List](appendix-c-reading-list.md).
